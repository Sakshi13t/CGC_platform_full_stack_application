# Cracked Gas Compressor Operating Procedure (OP-CGC-12)
*Synthetic internal procedure — for assignment use only.*

## 1. Normal operating envelope
- Suction pressure stage 1: 1.25 - 1.40 bar a
- Final discharge pressure: 35 - 38 bar a
- Rotational speed: minimum governor speed to maximum continuous speed (see asset register)
- Minimum anti-surge margin: 8% at all operating points; recycle valve modulates to hold margin
- Interstage cooled gas temperature target: cooling-water supply + 8 to 10 degC

## 2. Throughput / turndown
Turndown below 78% of design flow requires recycle (spillback) to maintain surge margin and
should be minimised because recycled gas is re-compressed and wastes energy. Prolonged
operation below 82% flow must be reported in the daily energy log.

## 3. Startup sequence (summary)
1. Establish lube oil and seal gas.
2. Confirm anti-surge valve fully open.
3. Roll turbine to minimum governor speed.
4. Load gradually while holding surge margin >= 12% during ramp.
5. Close recycle as flow establishes; target margin 8 - 15% at steady load.

## 4. Feed-slate changes
Switching from ethane to naphtha service increases gas molecular weight and diene content,
raising both compression duty and fouling rate. On a switch to heavier feed, increase
antifoulant dosing per RS-CGC-03 and shorten the performance-review interval to weekly.

## 5. Trips
The train trips on: high vibration (2oo3), high bearing metal temperature, low lube-oil
pressure, high axial displacement, or surge detection with failure of the anti-surge valve
to open. After a trip, do not restart until the cause is cleared and reliability engineering
authorises restart.
