# Energy Management Policy (EM-CGC-05)
*Synthetic internal policy — for assignment use only.*

## 1. Key performance indicator
The primary energy KPI for each CGC train is Specific Energy Consumption (SEC):
SEC = shaft power (kW) / throughput (t/h)  ->  kWh per tonne of cracked gas.

## 2. Targets (at comparable flow, clean machine)
- Ethane / light feed: SEC target <= 100 kWh/t
- Mixed feed: SEC target <= 106 kWh/t
- Naphtha / heavy feed: SEC target <= 112 kWh/t

A sustained SEC more than 8% above target at comparable flow indicates degradation and should
trigger a performance review (see RS-CGC-03).

## 3. Energy losses to manage
- Fouling: raises head and lowers efficiency; the single largest controllable energy loss.
- Recycle (spillback): re-compressing recycled gas is pure energy waste; minimise persistent recycle.
- Excess surge margin: running with unnecessarily high margin wastes energy; hold margin near,
  not far above, the 8% minimum.
- Interstage cooler underperformance: high interstage temperatures raise downstream duty.

## 4. Steam / driver
The trains are driven by extraction-condensing steam turbines. Steam cost is the dominant
operating cost. Reducing shaft power directly reduces steam consumption. Energy recommendations
should be expressed in both kW and equivalent steam cost where possible.

## 5. Trade-off with reliability
Energy optimisation must never compromise the surge-margin, vibration or temperature limits in
RS-CGC-01 and SP-CGC-04. When energy and reliability objectives conflict, reliability wins.
